1.1 Purpose & Vision

This document defines the formal software requirements for the Trinity Aviation Academy educational platform and CRM. The system serves candidate Aircraft Maintenance Engineers (AME) pursuing theoretical mastery of the EASA Part-66 syllabus (Modules 1 to 17) as well as certified maintenance personnel fulfilling mandatory recurrent training under EASA Part-145 and Part-M.

The platform is engineered around three core operational pillars:

Modular Study Hub
Integrated technical book readers (PDF simulator) and syllabus revision notes.
Random Exam Bank
Configurable sampling of $N$ questions per attempt with 75% EASA pass marks.
Bank Wire Workflow
Direct external transfer settlement with manual registrar validation.

External Bank Payment & Manual Approval Workflow

STEP 1
Course Selection

Candidate selects an EASA Part-66 or Part-145 subject. System opens the Payment Modal displaying price and bank transfer instructions.

STEP 2
External Bank Transfer

Candidate remits tuition via direct wire to the Academy bank account, quoting their auto-generated reference code (e.g. TRIN-ALE-8821).

STEP 3
Submit Wire Proof

Candidate enters their transaction reference. Order transitions to Pending Approval in "My Orders" and queues in the Admin matrix.

STEP 4
Admin Clearance

Registrar validates cleared funds, clicks Grant Access. Course materials unlock instantly and status updates to Completed.

Examination Engine Logic (Random Question Sampler)

1. Question Bank Administrator creates bank of $M$ questions with 4 options and 1 correct key.
2. Random Sampling Upon student launch, algorithm dynamically samples $N$ random questions without duplication.
3. Timed Session Live countdown timer runs; candidate can navigate via palette or flag items for review.
4. EASA Evaluation Answers evaluated against $75\%$ benchmark; confetti fires on pass; question breakdown audited.

Functional Requirements (FR) Matrix

Comprehensive traceability specifications categorized by subsystem
FR-PUB-01 Public Navigation
Top Navigation & Brand Identity

System shall provide a persistent top navigation bar with brand logo, links to Home, About Us, Services, Blogs, and a prominent Login / Sign Up action button.

FR-PUB-02 Curriculum Showcase
EASA Curriculum Preview Showcases

System shall showcase cards for EASA Part-145/Part-M courses and Part-66 modules with course title, syllabus code, description, duration, and tuition fee.

FR-PUB-05 Reviewer Utility
1-Click Demo Role Switcher

System shall supply a floating bottom-right widget allowing reviewers on Vercel to switch instantly between Student View (Alex Vance), Admin View (Hassaan Admin), and Landing Page.

FR-STU-01 Student Dashboard
Curriculum Progress Bar

System shall compute and render overall progress: (Enrolled Subjects / Total Available) * 100%, updating reactively upon access grant or enrollment.

FR-STU-04 Course Study Hub
3-Tab Subject Learning Interface

Enrolled subject view shall provide 3 dedicated tabs: Books (with simulated interactive PDF reader mode), Notes (chapter summaries and formulas), and Quizzes.

FR-STU-05 Exam Engine
Randomized Quiz Engine & Evaluation

System shall randomly extract $N$ questions from question bank per attempt, maintain countdown timer, evaluate against 75% pass mark, trigger celebratory confetti, and provide question-by-question review.

FR-PAY-01 Payment Protocol
Official Bank Remittance Presentation

When enrolling in locked course, system presents official bank details (Bank Name, Account Title, IBAN, SWIFT, Branch) and auto-generated unique reference code (e.g. TRIN-STU-XXXX).

FR-PAY-04 Order Tracking
Order Lifecycle & Certificates

Submitted bank proofs place order in Pending Approval. Once admin clears funds, order marks Completed and student can generate Certificate of Completion PDF.

FR-ADM-01 Admin KPI
Administrative Telemetry Dashboard

Renders high-level metrics (Total Registered Students, Active Subjects, Total Quiz Attempts, Pending Bank Approvals, Settled Tuition $) and alerts for pending orders.

FR-ADM-05 Content Authoring
4-Tab Course & Module Authoring

Admin modal supporting Basic details, Books (PDF upload simulator), Notes, and Quizzes with configurable question count and MCQ question bank editor.

FR-ADM-06 Audit Ledger
Quiz Attempts Audit Ledger

Audit log recording every student's quiz attempt with a "View Breakdown" modal revealing the exact questions served, student responses, and correct keys.

FR-ADM-08 Entitlement Matrix
3-Column Grant Access Workflow

Column 1 (Select Subject) -> Column 2 (No Access / Applied students with checkboxes & pending wire ref badges) -> Column 3 (Has Access with revoke). Batch activation reconciles pending orders.

Performance
  • NFR-PERF-01: Page Load & TTI Initial load under 1.5 seconds on mobile & broadband connections.
  • NFR-PERF-02: Client Latency Tab switching and modal opening in under 100ms.
  • NFR-PERF-03: Random Sampler Drawing $N$ random questions from bank in under 50ms.
  • NFR-PERF-04: Asset Bundle Production JavaScript bundle $\le 450 \text{ KB}$ gzipped.
Security & RBAC
  • NFR-SEC-01: Role Enforcement Strict separation between Student and Admin privileges.
  • NFR-SEC-02: Transport Encryption TLS 1.3 enforced across all network transmissions.
  • NFR-SEC-03: Input Sanitization All administrative and student inputs sanitized against XSS.
  • NFR-SEC-04: Key Concealment Answer keys concealed server-side until final submission.
EASA Compliance
  • NFR-REG-01: 75% Pass Standard Mandatory pass threshold aligned with EASA Part-66 Appendix II.
  • NFR-REG-02: MCQ Structure Exactly 4 options per question with 1 unambiguous correct key.
  • NFR-REG-03: Audit Trail Granular timestamped records of all test questions and responses.
